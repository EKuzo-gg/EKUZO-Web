# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Parents** of kids 10-18 who game. They decide and pay. Many are skeptical about gaming and screen time, and most aren't searching for youth esports: they're looking for something for a kid who games a lot, wants friends, or doesn't fit team sports.
- **Kids and teens 10-18** (players). They need it to feel fun and real, never like a class.
- **Schools and partners** evaluating EKUZO Teams and partnerships. They need legitimacy and structure.

## Product Purpose

EKUZO is coach-led youth esports: it puts a real coach and a real team around the gaming kids already do. Programs: EKUZO Teams (school-based, 15 weeks, 2-3x weekly), EKUZO 100 (4 weeks at home, 2x weekly), EKUZO Camps (seasonal week-long), EKUZO 101 (pilot), and Season 01 (remote league for ages 10-18, sold through ads at `/season1`). Success is a parent enrolling, joining a waitlist or starting a conversation, and a kid who feels they have a team.

## Positioning

Every gamer deserves a team. EKUZO adds structure and a coach to screen time, and claims the social-emotional lane (teamwork, communication, emotional regulation, confidence, belonging) rather than grades, scholarships or going pro.

## Operating Context

Parents usually meet EKUZO on a phone, from an ad, a referral or search, often skimming between other things. Kids are on PCs and in game. Registration and payment run through custom Stripe Elements checkout on the site; confirmations and prep come from Klaviyo; nurture from Beehiiv.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Commerce is live for Camps, Teams and EKUZO 100; Season 01 is a waitlist and ad funnel.
- FAQ answers come from `knowledge-base/company/knowledge/ekuzo-faq-canon.md`, never hand-written in pages.
- Existing pages, especially torn-paper seams and the color-flipping nav, are not refactored to match docs.

## Brand Commitments

- Name and wordmark EKUZO; tagline "Every gamer deserves a team."
- Visual system: `docs/EKUZO-BRAND.md` and `DESIGN.md`; off-site, the EKUZO Design System artifact (https://claude.ai/artifact/QMKPYkNS5EN23sVBYhfP1T).
- Voice: confident, not hype; plain and direct; no em dashes; no scholarship, GPA or go-pro claims; no emoji in product or marketing UI. "Gamer" stays in live copy; avoiding it in cold parent ads is a judgment call. CTAs from the parent's point of view ("Enroll my gamer", "Start a conversation").
- Season 01 is a deliberate sub-brand with its own look.

## Evidence on Hand

Real photos and footage of EKUZO kids, coaches and parents (media waivers on file; families are told before release), 9 testimonial videos in `public/testimonial-videos/`, brand collages in `public/images/`. League of Legends screenshots and pro arena or stock photos are third-party IP and never published. AI-generated images of kids carry a visible AI-generated note. No outcome statistics exist: don't invent proof numbers.

## Product Principles

1. Earn the parent's trust first, then let the kid's fun show.
2. Show the moment, don't argue the point.
3. Structure is the proof: seasons, coaches, teams, schedules.
4. Never overpromise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Family audience on phones: WCAG 2.1 AA. White on brand red only at large sizes. Inclusion is structural, never a label or a separate track.
