## Hard factual drifts (contradictions — decide the truth, then sync everywhere)

These are cases where the site contradicts itself or its own schema. LLMs will surface the inconsistency.

1. **Season length: 15 vs 16 weeks.**
   `/programs/ekuzo-teams`, `/programs`, and canon all say "~15 weeks." The `Course` schema says Fall 2026 runs Aug 31 → Dec 18 (~16 weeks). The schema is date-anchored, so it is probably the accurate one.
   *Fix:* pick one ("about 16 weeks, following the school calendar"), update Teams page + Programs index + canon + verify `lib/schema.ts`.

2. **Teams sessions per week: "two" vs "2–3".**
   `/programs/ekuzo-teams` says "typically two 90-minute sessions." Canon, `/programs`, `/schools`, and `/faq` all say "2–3 per week."
   *Fix:* change the Teams page to "2–3" (or correct the other four if two is right).

3. **Teams pricing is abstract everywhere it appears.**
   Teams page: "monthly or up front for a 10% discount… ~$20/session." `/faq` hub: "varies by format — visit the program pages." But the register page and schema commit to real numbers: **$576 paid in full (10% off), or 4 × $160 = $640.**
   *Fix:* put the dollars on the Teams page and the `/faq` hub. "How much does X cost" is the highest-intent commercial query an LLM gets; an abstract answer forfeits the citation.
