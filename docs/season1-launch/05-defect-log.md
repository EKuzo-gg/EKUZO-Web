## Not defects, recorded for the ledger

- Third-party hosts (fonts, gtag, Meta, Clarity) were stubbed or blocked in the harness; the only third-party console errors were from those blocked hosts.
- In the 375px full-page screenshots the landing's sticky "Save their spot" bar renders mid-page. That is a full-page capture artifact (fixed element) and is identical in the reference.
- On non-secure origins `crypto.randomUUID` is absent and the fallback ID (`<ms>-<random>`) is used; on https production it will be a UUID. Both dedupe correctly (the same value goes to the browser Lead and the API).
- Live requirements (L1 to L4) were not tested here: no network calls to Klaviyo or Meta, per instructions. The pre-flight flag still stands: L2 must confirm list membership (not only the event), since the route logs a subscribe failure and still returns ok.
