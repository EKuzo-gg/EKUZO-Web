## Pushy / contradictory / kid safety
- Mildly pushy: four "Save their spot" buttons, and "Share and save" above my receipt.
- A stranger with a crew link sees my kid's first name and "ages 10 to 17", nothing more. Good. But "Joining through Zoë's link tells us they belong together" suggests anyone with the link can ask to be on my kid's team. Say placement is still vetted.
- Ages 9 and 18 are selectable and can still pay ("Karlin will check in"); say it's refunded if it's not a fit.

## Top 3 fixes
1. One refund-and-jersey story: a full refund that is full (or the $65 rule on the price card), one jersey rule, and no "DRAFT" on the terms.
2. Exits that agree: the back arrow and the logo's home popup should point to open Season 01, and stop calling the free email list "Save their spot."
3. On the join page: practice nights and length, kid accounts and chat safety, and real coach photos.
