# Welcome email (thumbs)

Author: Story. Status: ready to paste into Beehiiv. Used for both A/B arms, per 00-brief.md § Locked decisions.

Sent to both A/B arms, so the opener names no promise that only the thumbs arm received. Two links carry the whole email. `{{THUMBS_UP_URL}}` goes to the newest post; `{{unsubscribe_url}}` is Beehiiv's unsubscribe merge tag (Relay confirms the exact tag name in pre-flight and swaps it if Beehiiv spells it differently). Do not add an image, a logo band, or a second call to action. The email is the promise being kept, and anything else in it makes the promise look smaller.

---

## Subject

```
One email. Thumbs up or thumbs down.
```

## Preheader

```
Thumbs down and you're off the list, no questions. Thumbs up and you keep it.
```

## Body (paste as paragraphs)

You left your address on the EKUZO blog, so here is the first email.

Thumbs up: [Keep it coming]({{THUMBS_UP_URL}}) takes you to the newest piece, and I read the click as a yes.

Thumbs down: [Take me off the list]({{unsubscribe_url}}) does exactly that. No questions, no follow-up.

You are already on the list, so the thumbs down is the only link that changes that. A short list of parents who want the weekly is worth more to me than a long one.

Karlin

Karlin Oei, founder, EKUZO

## Footer line

You are getting this because you left your address on the EKUZO blog. Unsubscribe any time: [{{unsubscribe_url}}]({{unsubscribe_url}})
