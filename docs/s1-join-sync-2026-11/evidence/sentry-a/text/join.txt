You pay $100 today for the first month deposit. The rest of the season is $160/month. That's $580 for the whole season, which runs January to May, about $20 a practice.
Do you offer discounts?
Yes! Siblings are 20% off. If a friend signs up with your unique link, you both get a free month. If 4 friends sign up, you play the season for free.
What game do they play?
Your kid might play Roblox, Minecraft or Fortnite, and they keep those. With us, they play League of Legends on a team of five. It’s free to download, and most kids start from scratch. League is our core game, and we’re looking at adding others.
Do we have to go anywhere?
No. Every practice and the final tournament happen online, from home.
Is it safe?
Every coach is background-checked, every practice is recorded, and you can drop in on any practice.
Who is it for?
Kids ages 9 to 17. Outside that range?

What if no team fits?
We hand-pick every team by age and skill. If we can't place your kid, you get every dollar back.
What do they need?
A computer that runs League of Legends (it's free), a headset with a mic, and solid internet. Our coach will help your player make sure they have everything set up before the first practice.
