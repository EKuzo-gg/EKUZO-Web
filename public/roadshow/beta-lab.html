Beta lab

BETA is a free two weeks before Season 01 with a pro coach: play on a real team, build community, have fun. The ads are getting clicks, but paying is too big a first step. So the email, the ads and the page now ask for one small thing: join our free beta with an email. Families see what we do before they decide.

Next step Karlin sends the email below to the 400+ families who signed up for Season 01.

The pathway

Two ways in, an ad or Karlin's email. Both end with an email address, which gets the wizard invite.

  1. Beta adFour new ads on Meta. Each lands on the BETA page, where a parent joins with one email field.Open the page
  2. Karlin's emailA personal note to the 400+ Season 01 sign-ups. To join, they just reply. Parents who reply yes get the beta launch page, whose button opens the wizard.Open the beta launch page
  3. Wizard inviteA short email with one link. Reply-yes parents get the beta launch page. Page sign-ups can go straight to the wizard.See the emails
  4. Sign-up wizardStarts with the parent's email, then three steps: what the beta is, a player card, and availability. It ends with a reaction test for the kid.Open the wizard
  5. Review and confirmWe check that each player's availability and our volume line up, then follow up with practice. How we confirm a player is still open.
  6. The betaTwo weeks, four sessions. Then the Season 01 offer.
