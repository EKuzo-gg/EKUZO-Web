Beta lab

A free four-week beta before Season 01: one evening practice a week, a small group, and families we know are a good fit. Committed families are in, and they pass it to friends who are still deciding.

Next step Karlin sends the interest email below to committed and interested families.

The email

Replies do the work of an application, so there is no form or page to build first.

A free EKUZO beta before Season 01

KKarlin from EKUZO, to [First name]

Hi [First name],

Before Season 01 starts, we're running a free 4-week beta: one evening practice a week with our coaches, playing as a squad.

We're keeping the group small on purpose, and we want to make sure it's a great fit for your player.

If you've already signed up for Season 01, you're in. Just reply with the weeknights that work best for your player.

If you haven't signed up yet and want to be considered, reply and tell me a little about your player:

  • Their first name and age
  • What they play right now
  • What you'd most like them to get out of it
  • Which weeknights work best for your family

We'll set the practice night based on what works for the group and follow up with everyone soon.

Know a family whose player would be a good fit? Feel free to pass this along.

Thanks,
Karlin

Send it as a personal emailFrom Karlin's own inbox, not a newsletter blast. It reads as a real invite and replies come straight to him.

Committed families reply tooTheir weeknights decide the practice night, so everyone weighs in.
