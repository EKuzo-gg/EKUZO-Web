For teachers and staff

Why a coach and a team
change how kids play.

Your students already game. Here's what changes when an adult coaches them.

“This seems like a gap that we have that we could close.”

A microschool founder

Alone, kids play with strangers until someone makes them stop. On a coached team, the same game becomes practice for things school cares about.

The same game, two ways

Gaming alone

  • Plays until someone says stop
  • Strangers in voice chat
  • A loss means raging or quitting

Gaming on an EKUZO team

  • A set start and a set end
  • Teammates they know, and a coach
  • Losses get reviewed, not raged

What students practice

  • Talking clearly under pressure
  • Handling a loss
  • Leading a teammate
  • Asking for help
  • Showing up on time

What staff see

  • A set time with a clear end
  • Notes from the coach
  • A showcase where families watch
  • A student who belongs to something

Structure, added

We add structure around something kids already love.
