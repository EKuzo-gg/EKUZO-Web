For university gaming clubs

Make the next class
as passionate as yours.

Your members coach kids, reach back to their old high schools, and grow your club's name.

“We're passionate. We want the next classes to be as passionate.”

Officer, a university League of Legends club

Clubs worry about who comes next, and about the stigma around their game. EKUZO brings younger players up through a coached league, so the next generation arrives knowing how to be a good teammate.

The loop your club is part of

Joins an EKUZO team
Kids 9 to 13

Plays for a school club
In their teens

Joins your club
In college

Coaches the next kids
Then the loop repeats

EKUZO brings

  • Paid coaching work, trained by us
  • A reach-back kit for your old high schools
  • Features of your players in our ads
  • A monthly event in front of families
  • Younger players coming up in your game

Your club brings

  • Members who want to coach
  • A post or flyer to your members
  • Showing up at one monthly event
  • Introductions to your old high schools

A light lift

Coaches, audience and one monthly event. That's the whole ask.

Your club stays yours

Nothing we run competes with your club or your roster.

Coaches with backup

A fixed pair of coaches per team, with your club as backup.

First step: Karlin meets your club

Our founder, a former collegiate League player, joins a club meeting online or at the arena to tell his story. Members who want to coach or reach back raise a hand.

Karlin Oei founded EKUZO. He was the anxious kid who found his people through games, and college esports helped pay for his school.

All EKUZO coaches are top 1% collegiate esports athletes or former professional players.
