How this works

  • Your edits stay in your browser until you send them. Close the tab and come back, they're still here. Nobody else sees them until you press Send my notes.
  • Send my notes sends Aaron your name, your Keep and Rework calls, every note, and your full version of the email with what changed. He folds it into the page and pushes it live for everyone.
  • "Show what changed" puts the current version under each line you edited, so you can see your changes before sending.
  • Getting it into Klaviyo: once an email is final, Aaron asks Claude to push it, or uses Copy Klaviyo HTML. An email already running in a flow keeps its own copy, so pick the template again in the flow and paste the subject and preview.
  • House rules for every email: from Karlin, in his voice. "Something they already love" means gaming, never the game their kid plays now. No scholarship promises. One question in the P.S. No em dashes.
