How this works

  • Your notes stay in your browser until you send them. Close the tab and come back, they're still here. Nobody else sees them until you press Send my notes.
  • Want to rewrite a line yourself? Turn on Edit above the preview. Every line, photo and block opens up, and the email updates as you type.
  • Send my notes sends Aaron your name, how the email feels to you, your note, and any edits you made. He folds them in and pushes the new version live for everyone.
  • House rules for every email: from Karlin, in his voice. "Something they already love" means gaming, never the game their kid plays now. No scholarship promises. One question in the P.S. No em dashes.
