    How this works

    One video, three placements. Reels and Stories show the full vertical video. The 4:5 and 1:1 feed cuts crop it. Use the crop position to keep faces in frame, and the cut gets exported that way.

    The red bands are approximate. Instagram puts the account name, caption and button over the bottom third and its controls along the top and right. Keep text and faces out of those areas.

    The click lands on an existing Season 01 page. The link carries utm_content with the video and version, so Jamie can see which cut brought each parent in.

    Notes stay in your browser until you send them. Send my notes sends Aaron your name, your call, your note, and the version, placement, crop and copy you had set. Nothing goes live from this page.
