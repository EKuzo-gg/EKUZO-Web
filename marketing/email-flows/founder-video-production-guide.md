# Founder Video Production Guide

Tools and recommended setups for shooting founder-led video content. A living doc — add notes as we learn what works.

---

## Recommended Starter Stack

**Total spend under $60. From script to posted video in ~30 minutes once you've done it once.**

1. **Shoot:** Phone + BIGVU app + a $40 teleprompter rig (Parrot, Glide Gear, or Desview from Amazon)
2. **Caption:** Submagic for AI auto-captions tuned for social
3. **Post:** Direct upload to your platforms

This is the realistic stack for the 30-second founder format we're producing. Anyone on the team should be able to run it.

---

## Phone Shooting (Default Setup)

**BIGVU** — best all-in-one. Teleprompter, recording, auto-captions, and an eye-level prompter overlay so the script reads as if it's behind the lens. ~$15/mo. Probably all you need on day one.

**PromptSmart Pro** — the smartest standalone teleprompter. Listens to your voice and only scrolls when you do. If you pause, it pauses. Huge difference vs. dumb scrolling speed. Worth pairing with BIGVU or using on its own.

**Hardware: phone teleprompter rig (~$30–$50)** — puts the screen in front of the lens so you appear to be looking right at the camera. This is what separates a "founder video" from a "person reading off-camera." Brands to look at: Parrot, Glide Gear, Desview.

---

## Webcam / Laptop Shooting

**Riverside.fm** — records at studio quality, built-in teleprompter, edit-by-transcript afterward. What most founders use for talking-head content right now.

**Descript** — same transcript-based editing, plus an AI eye-contact feature that corrects your gaze toward the camera even when you're reading off-screen. Genuinely useful for this exact use case.

---

## Captions and Quick Edits (Post-Shoot)

**Submagic** — AI auto-captions tuned for social. Adds the bouncing-word style that performs well on Reels and TikTok. Lowest friction.

**Captions.ai** — similar to Submagic, slightly different style options. Worth trying both and picking based on which captions you prefer.

**CapCut** — free, full control, slightly steeper learning curve. Use this when you want more than just captions (transitions, music, B-roll cuts).

---

## When to Use Which Setup

| Goal | Setup |
|---|---|
| Quick paid-social ad on phone | Phone + BIGVU + Submagic |
| More cinematic / polished | Riverside + Descript |
| Quick internal update / team video | Loom or webcam + BIGVU |
| Reading off teleprompter while looking like you're not | Phone teleprompter rig + PromptSmart Pro |

## Tips We're Learning

*(Add to this as we shoot more.)*

- Energy drops on every read. Push 20% past what feels natural — it'll look normal on camera.
- Land the closer with conviction. "No toxicity, ever. Just great games." is a promise, not a feature — pace it slowly.
- Look at the lens, not the screen. The teleprompter rig solves 90% of this, your awareness solves the other 10%.
- Shoot multiple takes per script. The third take is almost always the best one — you're past the nerves and not yet bored.
- Record vertical (9:16) by default. You can crop to horizontal in post, but you can't go the other direction without quality loss.
