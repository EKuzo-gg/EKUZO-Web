## Notes for the Shoot

- All scripts run ~75–85 words, which lands at 30 seconds with natural pacing (don't rush).
- Swap `[Name]` for the founder's actual name.
- The anchor line across every version is the **5-person team + elite coaches** combo — that's what tells parents this isn't just a gaming babysitter.
- "No toxicity, ever. Just great games." is the standard closer — it's a promise, not a feature list. Land it with conviction.
- For paid social, the bridge hook (Version A) is the strongest cold-audience opener because it instantly places EKUZO in a world parents already recognize.
- For warm audiences (existing list, retargeting), Version 3 (Reframe) or Version 1 (Parent Worry) tend to convert better — they speak to objections directly.

## Hook Recommendations by Audience

- **Cold paid social (parents):** Version A (Bridge) or Version F (Parent Convenience)
- **Cold paid social (kids watching alongside):** Version 2 (Kid's Dream)
- **Warm retargeting:** Version 1 (Parent Worry) or Version 3 (Reframe)
- **Organic / founder content:** Original or Version 5 (Curiosity)
- **Out-of-market / national reach:** Version E (Anywhere in the Country)
- **Friend groups / squads:** Version I (Summer Plans) or Version G (Bring Your Squad)
- **Solo players / no squad yet:** Version H (Find Your Team)
- **Single ad serving both audiences:** Version J (Two Ways In)
- **Parents worried about screen time:** Version O (Real Choice) or Version N (Tutor Frame)
- **Skeptical / "is this just gaming?" parents:** Version P (Actual Camp) or Version Q (Schedule)
- **Parents who already accept gaming (validate + level up):** Version V (Already Learning) or Version R (Direct)
- **Strongest standalone persuasive piece:** Version T (Manifesto with Skills)
