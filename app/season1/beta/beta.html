Short answers

Is it really free?

Yes. Two weeks, four sessions, no card, and no commitment to Season 01. We want your family to see what we do before you decide anything.

What happens after I join?

We email you a few quick questions about your player and the times that work. Then we send your practice times.

What happens after the beta?

Season 01 starts in mid January. If your player loved the beta, they can join a team for the season. If it wasn't for them, you owe nothing.

What game do they play?

Your kid might play Roblox, Minecraft or Fortnite, and they keep those. With us, they play League of Legends on a team of five. It’s free to download, and most kids start from scratch.

What do they need?

A computer, a headset or headphones, and a mouse. Any laptop or Mac will work, and League of Legends is free. We send tech setup instructions later.

Do we have to go anywhere?

No. Every practice happens online, from home.

Who is it for?

Kids ages 9+, at any skill level.
