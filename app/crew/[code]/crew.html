What is EKUZO

Every gamer
deserves a team.

They're already gaming. We give it a point. A real coach, the same teammates every week, and a season to play for. Kids practice communication, strategy and leadership, the kind of skills that show up at school and on every team they'll ever join.

Screen time becomes practice time. And you get something real to ask about at dinner.

How a season works

A real team.
From home.

01

Practice with a coach

Two evenings a week. A real coach runs every practice: teamwork, communication, strategy.

02

Play with their team

The same five teammates all season, hand-picked by age and skill. Friends stay together wherever we can.

03

Play for something

A team jersey they design together, a schedule, and a final tournament in May.

The game

League of Legends plays like a sport.

Five players, set roles, one shared goal. It's free and runs on most family computers. Your kid can keep their Roblox, Minecraft or Fortnite. No League experience needed; most kids start from scratch.
